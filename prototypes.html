    Each step feeds its chosen pattern and words into the next step. Changing an earlier result changes how later stored choices are read; it does not guarantee that every later word will differ.

    What can I write in this experiment?

    This small grammar demonstrates nine patterns. It uses the existing name and adjective lists, but it does not support all the notes the main app accepts. It allows up to 64 clauses, ending in periods or question marks.

    • this is cool.
    • sarah waits.
    • i love you.
    • sarah travels to new york.
    • the doctor is in paris.
    • we might dance.
    • does sarah know me?
    • tomorrow, sarah arrives.
    • sarah, you are sexy.

    Names and places containing periods are excluded from this experiment. Verb choices are deliberately small. Every output follows these patterns, but its meaning may be unusual. Clause count stays fixed. These are research prototypes, not a security-reviewed replacement for the main app.
