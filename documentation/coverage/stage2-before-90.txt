WHAT CHANGED
Reviewed names and lexical roles now include enron, erasmus, common calendar
names, attached, into, put, additional common verbs and inflections, nouns and
function words. Put requires an object plus a location; words were not simply
put in a generic verb pool. Existing unknown spellings remain failures.
Balanced parentheses/brackets/quotes, dashes, semicolon-separated utterances,
integer/grouped/cent monetary amounts, and padded 12-hour times have explicit
reversible layouts. Unmatched delimiters, invalid times and malformed amounts
are negative controls. No arbitrary text or punctuation is passed through.
