A NOTE WITH A TWIST

A little message.
A different unfolding.

One link. A different story for every passkey.
Yours brings back the words you put inside.

A little guidance

01 / THE KEY

Keep the generated name or choose your own passkey. Open a shared link and enter a passkey to unfold its message.

02 / THE WORDS

Write in the message box. Uppercase becomes lowercase; unsupported words or grammar appear in red. Try “hello. i love you.”

03 / THE FOLD

Generate a link and copy it to share. The same passkey brings back your message. A different passkey unfolds a different reading.

Edit a received message to start a new note. Spaces and line breaks are normalized when you generate a link.
