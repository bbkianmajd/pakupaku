Encoder and decoder demo

Write a note and create a link. Your key recovers the note; other keys can change its words and sentence structures.

Create a link


      
    

    

    
Uppercase becomes lowercase as you type. Spaces and line breaks are normalized in the link. Unsupported words or grammar are highlighted in red. Try hello, i love you, sarah, you are sexy., or hello. i love you. can you help me? meet me at 1530.

    
Broad grammar mode allows unusual combinations such as sarah arrives the sandwich., while checking word forms and agreement.

    
    
    
Replace the generated key with one you choose.

    
    

    
    
  

  

    
Read the link

    
    
    
Every reader key produces a reading, including an empty key. Sentence structures and words can change, with earlier choices influencing later ones.
